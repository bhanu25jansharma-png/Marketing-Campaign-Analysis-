# Marketing Campaign Analysis
A collection of Data Analytics projects completed during my Elevate Labs Data Analyst Internship, showcasing practical experience in Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python, and other data analytics tools.
Author:- Bhanu Pratap

**Project Overview**
This project focuses on analyzing a comprehensive dataset of **10,000+ digital marketing campaigns** to evaluate performance, optimize budget allocation, and maximize Return on Investment (ROI). Built entirely using **Microsoft Excel**, the project utilizes advanced data cleaning, pivot tables, and multi-sheet aggregation to derive actionable business insights.

**Dataset Features & Schema**
The dataset tracks key digital marketing metrics across multiple dimensions:
* Campaign Metadata: Campaign ID, Name, Type (Awareness, Retargeting, Sales), Marketing Channel, Start & End Dates.
* Geographic & Audience Data: Region, City, Target Audience, Device Type (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop).
* Engagement & Performance: Impressions, Clicks, CTR (%), Conversions, Conversion Rate (%), Customers Acquired.
* Financial Metrics: Campaign Cost, Revenue Generated, ROI (%).

## Tools & Techniques Used
* Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, conditional formatting, text standardization, and missing value checks.
* Pivot Tables & Summarization: Grouping data by channels, regions, and campaign types.
* Financial Calculations: Dynamic formulas for ROI percentage, cost per acquisition, and conversion ratios.

## Key Findings & Insights
1. Revenue Drivers: **Meta Ads** and **Google Ads** generate the highest total revenue share due to massive impression volumes and broad audience reach.
2. Channel Cost Efficiency: Average campaign costs remain stable across channels (averaging around \$3,800 to \$4,020), with **LinkedIn** and **Meta Ads** showing strong cost-to-acquisition ratios for B2B/targeted segments.
3. Campaign Themes: Campaigns like **Summer Sale** and **Festive Offer** accumulated the highest total impressions (over 300M+ combined), driving substantial top-of-funnel traffic.
4. ROI Distribution: While high-spend channels drive raw revenue, targeted campaigns yield high-leverage ROI percentages, highlighting the importance of balancing broad awareness with retargeting strategies.
